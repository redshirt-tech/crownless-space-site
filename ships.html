Ship Systems

The Beagle (Kodiak-class)

A refurbished Kodiak-class warship. These ships were mothballed after the Zookeeper war for being unsuited to peacetime, but their overengineered power systems and single-piece, Solaran-made hyperdiamond superstructure made them ideal for repurposing as long-range exploration and patrol ships.

The refit stripped the advanced weapons systems, heat sinks, beam capacitors, and redundant shield arrays — leaving ample cargo space — and added a new protomatter reactor and state-of-the-art FTL torii drive. The ship retains a basic beam weapon, shield array, and spartan crew amenities. Ship and crew can be upgraded with additional Requisition.
