Ship Systems

The Beagle (Kodiak-class)

A refurbished Kodiak-class warship, repurposed as a long-range exploration and patrol ship (see [The Burden of Command] for the refit history). Each system below lists its full description and mechanical effects.

Tactical

Command Bridge

The bridge is the command center for the ship. It has several crew stations for controlling various parts of the ship and relaying operational information, as well as a large viewscreen-window for showing data or communications to everyone on the bridge. Also included are the conference room, the captain's ready room, and a small break room with a basic food synthesizer and autochef. The ship can theoretically be run entirely from the bridge, although in practice there is often a need for crew present in other parts of the ship for maintaining and calibrating the various technical systems.

  • Mechanical Effect: The bridge must be operational and crewed in order for the ship to function.
  • Crew Effect: Must have a crew team for ship to function.
  • Damage Effect: All ship rolls −1.
  • Upgrade: Battle bridge. During ship combat or other emergencies, the ship can be run from a reinforced and ruggedized control center, preventing disruption due to damage.

Security

The security department hosts the security offices, the security team's quarters, the armory, and a small brig capable of detaining individuals for short periods of time. From here, the security team inventories and maintains the ship's small arms, as well as plans security drills to keep the crew prepared in case of emergency. The security team is also in charge of investigating security incidents, such as potential sabotage or violent misconduct.

  • Mechanical Effect: Houses a security team that will defend the ship from external threats, and accompany away missions on request.
  • Crew Effect: The security team resides in the security department unless active elsewhere. Security cannot be crewed by non-security crew teams.
  • Damage Effect: No access to weapons or armor. Cannot hold prisoners in the brig.
  • Upgrade: Additional security team.

Particle Beam Cannon

A basic ship-to-ship weapon, largely similar to the weapons used to fight the Zookeepers. It fires a powerful beam of charged particles with great precision, and is effective at distances of up to a light-second before bloom substantially diminishes the effects of the beam (this bloom effect is increased substantially if firing through an atmosphere). It is equally effective against shields and hull plating.

  • Mechanical Effect: 2d6 damage on successful hit.
  • Crew Effect: Free reroll for each crewed weapon.
  • Damage Effect: Damage −2.
  • Upgrade: Damage +2.

Shuttle Bay

The shuttle bay contains two shuttlecraft capable of ferrying passengers or cargo to and from the ship. The shuttle bay has room for the shuttles and all the tools and equipment to maintain them.

A shuttle has six seats with control stations in the cockpit, and the rear of the craft is modular. It can be configured for short-range cargo hauling with space roughly equivalent to a standard cargo container, short-range passenger transport with seats for 20 people, or long-range transit capable of housing six people for several days. A shuttle can be reconfigured in about eight hours with a full team.

  • Mechanical Effect: Access to a shuttlecraft for transport to and from the ship.
  • Crew Effect: Reconfigure a shuttle.
  • Damage Effect: The shuttle cannot be launched.
  • Upgrade: Gain a second shuttle.

Operations

Reactor

A protomatter reactor functions by subjecting a pellet of normal matter to extreme pressure, conditions similar to the Big Bang, at which point the fundamental forces unify and the matter converts into a quark-gluon plasma, aka protomatter. As the protomatter cools and expands, the phase-change of the unified force breaking down releases more energy than was applied to compress it. This creates negative pressure within the reaction chamber, and thus negative energy, which can be turned into useful energy to power the ship, or redirected to open wormholes and power tachyon comms and sensors.

  • Mechanical Effect: Generates power for the ship's systems.
  • Crew Bonus: Generates a Power token that can be used to boost the effectiveness of another system.
  • Damage Effect: No Power tokens, non-essential systems are shut down to conserve power.
  • Upgrade: Generate 1 additional Power token when crewed.

Higgs Drive

The Higgs Drive mechanism contains exotic island-of-stability elements that have spacetime-altering properties. These particles are cycled through coiled accelerators at relativistic speeds, effectively "whipping" spacetime locally, creating a bubble of extremely thin spacetime that fixes everything inside of it to a single inertial reference frame relative to space outside the bubble. This bubble can then be charged with negative energy, canceling out the ship's Higgs field and lowering its effective inertial mass to near-zero. This allows the entire bubble to be propelled to relativistic speeds at arbitrarily high accelerations using conventional high-impulse thrusters.

This is much more energetically efficient for moving ships than accelerating and decelerating at full mass. This does not allow for superluminal propulsion, as it would still take an infinite amount of energy to accelerate to light speed, but it's extremely useful for orbital maneuvering or traveling between locations within a star system.

  • Mechanical Effect: Allows conventional sublight travel.
  • Damage Effect: The ship cannot maneuver in real space, severely limiting its ability to travel within star systems.
  • Upgrade: More combat maneuverability.

Engineering

The Engineering department is where the ship's technical systems are monitored, maintenance is coordinated, and solutions are designed. This is the central control hub for the ship's power and propulsion systems.

  • Mechanical Effect: Central coordination hub for ship technical systems.
  • Crew Bonus: Must be crewed for ship to function.

Fabrication

The fabrication system, 'fabber' or 'fab' to some, is a catch-all term for a variety of automated manufacturing processes that can turn raw materials into useful goods and materials without the need for specialized factories or manual labour. They combine nano-scale additive manufacturing, CNC machines, robotic assembly, and even biomachinery to create advanced tools, devices, materials, parts, etc. based on designs provided by the user. Fabricator workshops are essential aboard deep space ships, as there may be a need for replacing parts or equipment while traveling the Void.

  • Mechanical Effect: Allows the crew to manufacture equipment and parts on-demand.

Torii Drive

With careful gravitic field manipulation, negative energy can be channeled into wormhole openings, stabilizing them and allowing the passage of information and even macro-objects through them. This allows travel from one point in space to another without crossing the space in between.

This principle is used by waygate stations to maintain dedicated gates between star systems, but the same principle can be used to propel a ship to an unknown destination through existing primordial wormholes. Wormholes require exponentially more negative energy to stabilize the further apart the openings are in space, so astrogators will guide the ship through many wormholes in rapid succession, achieving apparent superluminal speeds. This technique is often unworkable or impractical when near a gravity well due to the unevenness of spacetime, and so it's much more reliable to use sublight propulsion for travel within a star system.

The Torii Drive gets its name from torii gates, traditional Japanese gates often built at the entrance to Shinto shrines, and in particular the Row of a Thousand Gates at Fushimi Inari-taisha in Kyoto, Japan, where there are walking trails lined with rows of hundreds of torii gates.

Astrogation with the torii drive requires the ability to sense multidimensional space in order to determine the direction that a wormhole will lead, and so there must always be a sentient astrogator to make any intentional progress to a specific destination.

  • Mechanical Effect: Allows FTL travel.

Science

Shield Array

The deflector shield system creates powerful fields around to divert energy and particles away from the ship, both to protect from harm as well as to reduce drag when traveling at relativistic speeds. Deflectors are usually a combination of a strong electromagnetic field, gravity distortion field, and hard-light projection, although there are theories about the possibility of multidimensional fields. Shields are designed to protect against as many threats as possible, but none are completely impermeable, and modifications may need to be made depending on circumstances.

  • Mechanical Effect: Shields +1.
  • Crew Bonus: May roll Repair or Electronics once per combat round to regain 1 lost shield.
  • Damage Effect: Shields −1.
  • Upgrade: Shields +1.

Main Computer

The main computer is a massive array of stratified computronium, organized for maximum processing power and data storage relative to heat dissipation. While the computer is extremely powerful, it does have to serve the demands of multiple complex technical systems, and so it has limited surplus computing power. That surplus can be used to improve the performance of systems, or to automate a system in the absence of adequate crew.

  • Mechanical Effect: One Compute token is available to be placed on any given system to either automate it as if there were a crew team assigned to it, or adds +1 to rolls associated with the system.
  • Crew Bonus: Uncrewed.
  • Damage Effect: Cannot use Compute tokens.
  • Upgrade: Additional Compute token.

Sensors

The standard energy emitter and receiver array is a combination of several overlapping systems designed to passively and actively gather data around the ship. It functions over a broad spectrum, with multi-frequency capabilities, able to resolve millimeter resolutions at distances of thousands of kilometers.

This also includes long-range sensors via negative energy tachyonic emissions, allowing for the ship to detect objects in real-time at distances of several light years. Tachyonic sensors are much less precise than the conventional sensors, more resembling ancient RADAR technology in their capabilities, but large objects such as spacecraft can generally be detected at interstellar distances.

  • Mechanical Effect: Must be online to pass sensor checks and detect things outside the ship.
  • Crew Bonus: Rolls involving the ship's sensors +1.
  • Damage Effect: The ship has no long-range sensors, and can only detect things in the immediate vicinity.
  • Upgrade: Rolls involving the ship's sensors +1.

Science Lab

A basic science laboratory has a broad set of tools and equipment used to conduct experiments and gather data on objects, samples, and artifacts brought aboard the ship. The lab is highly reconfigurable, but is no replacement for specialized labs dedicated to specific fields of study.

  • Mechanical Effect: Allows players to make Science and Research rolls to learn about novel scientific discoveries.
  • Crew Bonus: Crew may roll Science or Research without need for PCs.
  • Damage Effect: Cannot use science lab.
  • Upgrade: Lab may specialize in specific areas of study, such as biology, geology, or archaeology, granting a free reroll to rolls involving those specialties.

Stellar Cartography

Stellar cartography is a purpose-built computer system and database designed to collect sensor data about stellar objects and catalogue them for reference. Data from stellar cartography is regularly uploaded to the Cooperative database to further the accuracy and precision of the Cooperative's starmaps.

  • Mechanical Effect: Allows mapping of sectors.
  • Crew Bonus: Must be crewed to function.
  • Damage Effect: Cannot map sectors.
  • Upgrade: Mapping roll +1.

Support

Crew Quarters

The basic crew quarters include a small single-occupant room with a bed, workstation, storage closet, and bathroom. Quarters can be reconfigured for double-occupancy at the cost of comfort and privacy. Each set of crew quarters has a shared common area for relaxing and socializing. Crew are not permitted to cook in their quarters for safety and ventilation reasons.

  • Mechanical Effect: Crew Quarters house 3 crew teams. Each additional crew team causes −1 Morale.

Crew Support Facilities

These are shared crew facilities such as a commissary, fitness center, recreation area, simulation bay, etc. These facilities are essential for maintaining crew morale on long voyages.

  • Mechanical Effect: Each additional support facility reduces Morale penalty by 1. Some may have additional roleplaying functions.

Life Support

Life support is a catch-all term for the systems that maintain appropriate environmental conditions for life aboard the ship. It includes air and water systems, climate control, gravity plating, and the monitoring and filtering of environmental contaminants. Life support systems use a combination of several overlapping mechanical and biological subsystems to ensure maximum reliability.

  • Mechanical Effect: Maintains livable environment for the crew.
  • Damage Effect: Life Support is highly decentralized and thus cannot be damaged directly. However, certain ship-wide effects may cause it to malfunction.

Medical Bay

The medical bay is a small hospital, complete with several medical beds, a couple of private offices for medical exams, a large storage room, and an advanced autodoc for automated diagnostics and robot-assisted surgery. It also has a medical-grade fabrication unit, complete with a pharmaceutical synthesizer and microbiotic engineering capabilities. It is best suited for treating acute conditions and stabilizing patients long enough to get them to more comprehensive medical treatment facilities.

  • Mechanical Effect: Facilities for injured crew. The autodoc can make a single Healing roll at d4 up to 24 hours after a Wound, rather than within the Golden Hour.
  • Crew Bonus: The medical team can make regular Healing rolls in addition to the autodoc, as well as in support of the autodoc.
